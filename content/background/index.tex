\chapter{Background and Related Work}
As stated in the introduction, the high computational cost of PCA introduces a significant challenge for real-time anomaly detection, especially as the volume of data grows really large. To address this limitation, \cite{rakuschek2025visual} proposed cleverly combining Time Delay Embedding (TDE) with PCA in their paper Visual Fingerprints of Vibration Signals Using Time Delay Embeddings. Their implementation generates visual plots from vibration data that resemble human fingerprints. This chapter explains the theoretical foundations of PCA and TDE in detail, identifies key computational bottlenecks, and reviews the original offline implementation of the algorithm.
