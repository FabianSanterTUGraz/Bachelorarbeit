\subsection{Combining vectors}
The method for combining three orthogonal acceleration vectors into a single composite measure is the vector magnitude approach, as it preserves the total acceleration energy of the signal. By only taking the sum or average of the three axes it may introduce bias and risks losing important information. To obtain a single measure of total acceleration, the vector magnitude is calculated using the formula:
\begin{equation}
    VM = \sqrt{(x^2 + y^2 + z^2)}
\end{equation}
This method is supported by \cite{formulaCombiningVectors}, who state that "the VM equation is the square root of the sum of the squares of three planes of motion."
